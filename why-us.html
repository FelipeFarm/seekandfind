Our story

Founded in 2018 to ask better questions.

Seek & Find was founded to give business owners a way to grow wealth and reduce taxes without feeling overwhelmed or pressured.

Our name comes from Matthew 7:7, and it reflects our mission: to ask better questions and find the right answers for each client.

Discovery first

We ask the right questions, and help you answer yours.

We start with your questions, not products or assumptions. These are personal questions tied to your lifestyle, legacy, and peace of mind, so we tailor strategies to your values.

  • Where should I put my money?
  • How much should I set aside, and where?
  • What’s the difference between my 401(k) and other investments?
  • Why do I need life insurance?
  • What the heck is an annuity?
  • I need cash. Where should I take it from?
  • How do I get my business ready for sale? And when I do, where do I go from there?
Fiduciary standard

We serve people, not products.

We are not tied to one investment model or product category. We evaluate a range of tools, including stocks, bonds, crypto, life insurance, and annuities.

As fiduciaries, we:

  • Always act in your best interest
  • Recommend what suits your situation, not our bottom line
  • Treat your finances as we would our own
